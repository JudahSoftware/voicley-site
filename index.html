Everything a voice deserves

Built around one law: capture first. Your words hit disk from the first syllable. The network, the engine, even the app can hiccup and you still lose nothing.

Live

Live as you talk

Words appear in well under a second, punctuated and clean. A streaming engine built for real time, so your thoughts keep pace with your mouth.

Reader · new

Reader & Now Playing

Line up folders and notes, pick the reading voice (natural or offline) and the speed. Now Playing follows word by word; tap any word to jump there; Resume drops you where you left off.

Listen · new

Dictate while it reads

Press play and the mic stays open. It reads a sentence, pauses to listen, and the moment you speak it stops for you, keeps the idea, then carries on. Built for the car.

Sort · new

It files itself

Every note is titled as you speak. The Inbox counts the notes that already have a place; one tap files them, with Undo on every move. Loose notes get sorted with AI, even into brand-new folders.

Clean up · new

Only your words stay

Voicley points out text in a note that is not yours, an ad it overheard or a page it read aloud, and you approve each cut. Long recordings are read in full before anything is suggested.

Re-Written · new

Your words, or tidied

One switch, per folder: your own words, or a tidied, rewritten version that tells you what it left out. Nothing is saved until you say so, and you can always go back.

Library · new

Favorites & Hidden

A star on every note, by hand or by voice. A Hidden place for the notes you keep but never want offered, and search that finds them when you ask.

Sync

Phone, PC, browser

Folders and sub-folders, and one idea can live in several at once. Text and audio sync across your phone, your PC and voicley.com/app, and your phone updates itself.

Archive

OneNote, live

Dictation can stream straight into your OneNote pages as you speak, titles, sections and all, so your archive fills itself.

Safety

Never loses a word

Local-first and fully offline-capable: audio is saved from the first frame. Dead network, a crash, a call mid-sentence: it recovers and re-transcribes. When the signal drops, an offline engine keeps live words rolling until it returns.

The capture-first promise. An app that drops your words is worse than useless: you stop trusting your own notes, and then you stop using it. Voicley writes audio to disk from the first frame, survives dead networks, mid-session crashes and phone calls, and re-transcribes anything that didn’t finish. If you said it, it’s saved.
